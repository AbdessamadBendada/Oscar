2. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device. Websites also use similar technologies, such as local storage, tracking pixels and scripts that read information from your device. On this page we call all of these "cookies".

3. The rules we follow

Article 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunications Act (Telecommunicatiewet) requires your prior consent for cookies, except for cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the website and analytics cookies with little or no impact on your privacy. Where cookies process personal data, the GDPR applies as well.

5. Your choices

On your first visit, a banner asks for your choice. You can accept all cookies, reject all optional cookies or choose per category. Rejecting takes the same single click as accepting, and no optional category is switched on in advance.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Withdrawal applies from that moment on and does not affect use that took place before. We remember your choice for [12 months] and then ask again.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking functional cookies may stop parts of the website from working.

6. Your rights

Where cookies involve personal data, you have the rights described in section 8 of our Privacy Policy, including the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Changes

We update this policy when we add or remove tools or when the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we add a category that needs consent, we ask for your consent again.
