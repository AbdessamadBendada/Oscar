The measure

Measure the delay without confusing the stages.

Truth Latency = Received − Known

Known

The earliest evidenced time someone recognised a relevant risk, problem or insight.

Received

When someone with appropriate authority received enough information to act.

Action

When a decision or concrete response began. This is tracked separately.

The route

Give the concern somewhere useful to go.

A clearer recipient, confirmation of receipt and an agreed response can shorten the route without ranking the messenger.

What changes

The route and the response.

Structure

Name a recipient with authority, a backup and an urgent route that works across roles and shifts.

Behaviour

Receive the concern with curiosity, clarify the facts, name the next step and close the loop.

Environment

Make written and private routes usable, and ensure remote voices can reach the same authority.
