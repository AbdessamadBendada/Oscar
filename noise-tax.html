What this is not

This is recovered capacity, not cash saved. Nobody leaves the payroll because three meetings stop. What you get back is hours, and hours turn into work the company would otherwise hire for, decisions that happen this week instead of next month, and evenings that belong to you.

It is also provisional. It rests on seven answers from one person, and that person is the one furthest from the floor. The next step is the part you cannot do alone.
