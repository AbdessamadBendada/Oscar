The problem

Familiaris not thesame as useful.

  • 01

    A meeting continues because it has always been there.

  • 02

    An approval remains long after the risk it protected has changed.

  • 03

    A report is produced without anyone choosing what it is for.

The method

Three moves.
In order.

Start with evidence. Protect what is useful. Remove what no longer earns its place. Then make one explicit choice the team can act on.

See

Recognise reality

Look at what actually happened, who is involved and which useful function the current practice still serves.

Strip

Remove the noise

Separate the useful work from the inherited format, duplicate effort and approval without purpose.

Choose

Decide and act

Keep, adjust or change the practice. Name the owner, the first move and when the choice will be reviewed.
