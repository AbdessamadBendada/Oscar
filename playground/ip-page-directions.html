The method

In order.

Start with evidence. Protect what is useful. Remove what no longer earns its place. Then make one explicit choice the team can act on.

The delay is not only whether someone speaks. It is whether useful information reaches someone with the authority to act.

The measure

Measure the delay without confusing the stages.

Truth Latency = Received − Known

Known

The earliest evidenced time someone recognised a relevant risk, problem or insight.

Received

When someone with appropriate authority received enough information to act.

Action

When a decision or concrete response began. This is tracked separately.

The route

Give the concern somewhere useful to go.

A clearer recipient, confirmation of receipt and an agreed response can shorten the route without ranking the messenger.

What changes

The route and the response.

Structure

Name a recipient with authority, a backup and an urgent route that works across roles and shifts.

Behaviour

Receive the concern with curiosity, clarify the facts, name the next step and close the loop.

Environment

Make written and private routes usable, and ensure remote voices can reach the same authority.

Proprietary framework · DRMT Model™

Make the
decision
travel.

A decision is useful when people can find what was chosen, why it was chosen, how it will be assessed and when it should be reviewed.

DecisionWhat we chose. Who owns it.
ReasonWhy this choice. What we assumed.
MetricWhat we watch. How we know.
TriggerWhen to review. Who responds.
The problem

Agreement in the room is not clarity afterwards.

Before

“We agreed to explore it.” People leave with different interpretations and reopen the discussion.

After

A specific choice, owner, rationale, useful measure and review condition are easy to find.

The model

Four lines.
One usable decision.

DRMT records the outcome. It does not replace decision rights, create authority or require consensus.

D

Decision

State the choice, named owner, scope and mandate.

R

Reason

Explain the priority, evidence, assumptions and trade-off.

M

Metric

Name an observable indicator, source and expected direction.

T

Trigger

Name the condition for review, who watches it and who can reconsider.

Illustrative record

From vague agreement to usable choice.

A weekly update is redesigned as a four-week trial. The record tells anyone who missed the meeting what happens next.

Decision

Trial a written Tuesday update for four weeks. Keep a live slot for unresolved decisions.

Reason

The current meeting repeats information already available in the tracker.

Metric

Compare weekly person-hours and check whether any decisions are missed.

Trigger

Review early if an urgent decision misses its response window or the update lacks context twice.
