The problem

Before

“We agreed to explore it.” People leave with different interpretations and reopen the discussion.

After

A specific choice, owner, rationale, useful measure and review condition are easy to find.

The model

Four lines.
One usable decision.

DRMT records the outcome. It does not replace decision rights, create authority or require consensus.

D

Decision

State the choice, named owner, scope and mandate.

R

Reason

Explain the priority, evidence, assumptions and trade-off.

M

Metric

Name an observable indicator, source and expected direction.

T

Trigger

Name the condition for review, who watches it and who can reconsider.

Illustrative record

From vague agreement to usable choice.

Trigger
